AI Summary Helper is a free, open-source Chrome extension that summarizes any web page, PDF or YouTube video in your language, with your own AI key or a fully local model through Ollama. It turns what you read into a private, searchable library with highlights, follow-up questions, a knowledge graph, reading analytics, an RSS reader and Send to Kindle. Your library can also be used from AI apps like Claude Desktop and Cursor. No account is needed for your own key or a local model, and your articles stay on your device.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
✨ WHAT IT DOES IN ONE MINUTE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Press Ctrl+Shift+E (⌘+Shift+E on Mac) or right-click → "Summarize this page".
• The result streams into the side panel, popup or an in-page sidebar, in any of 41 languages.
• Key sentences light up on the page as "ghost highlights", so you can jump straight to the evidence.
• Ask follow-up questions; answers quote the passage they come from.
• Everything is saved to your History: search it, star it, see how topics connect, export it.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
🔑 YOUR AI, YOUR CHOICE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Pick the model that fits your budget and your privacy needs:

• Own API key (BYOK): OpenAI, Google Gemini, Mistral AI or DeepSeek. You pay the provider per request, usually a few cents a month, instead of a flat subscription.
• Fully local with Ollama: run an open model on your own computer, or on a self-hosted server in your network. No API key, no cost per request, and page content never leaves your machine.
• byphil Cloud (optional): no key and no setup. Sign in with your email and a one-time code and get a small free daily allowance; upgrade to Pro for unlimited summaries.

Your own key and the on-device option need no account at all. The full source code is public on GitHub, so you can check exactly what the extension sends and where.

📄 WEB PAGES, PDFs AND VIDEOS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Web pages and news articles: clean extraction of the article text, without menus, ads or cookie banners.
• PDFs: research papers, reports and manuals opened in your browser, including files on your computer.
• YouTube videos: summaries built from the video's captions, so you can decide whether a 40-minute talk is worth your time.
• Length: "Auto" scales with the length of the article, or set a fixed length from 20 to 2,000 words, with a short, standard or long bias.
• Prompts: presets such as Technical Deep Dive, Historical Context, Cultural Perspective and Future Predictions, plus a guided "make it yours" builder that turns your role and interests into your own prompt.
• Summarize & close tab: get the key points, choose when to come back, and let the tab close itself.

🗂️ YOUR READING LIBRARY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Every summary is kept in your History on this device, organized into Inbox, Read, Sent, Research, Collections and Archive.

• Search: instant full-text search across all saved articles, on your device.
• Similar articles: see what else in your library is related to the article you are reading.
• Duplicate detection by link, title and content keeps the library clean.
• Saving from archive.ph, the Wayback Machine or 12ft keeps the original article link, so your library points at the real source.
• Collections: group articles under one question and work through them with a source-check worksheet.
• Favorites, tags, notes and timed reminders.

🕸️ KNOWLEDGE GRAPH
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
See how everything you have read connects. The knowledge graph groups your articles into topic clusters, links articles that share tags or content, and shows a reading list of topics you have not opened in 30 days. Write a short brief for any cluster and export it as Markdown.

📰 RSS READER WITH AI RECAPS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Follow blogs, newspapers and podcasts by RSS or Atom; import and export your subscriptions as OPML.
• Daily recaps that roll up into weekly and monthly overviews, written by the AI you chose. Only titles and snippets are sent, and only when you ask.
• With a local model, new items can be rated and recapped in the background every 15 minutes to 6 hours.
• Story stacks group the same story from different outlets.
• Feed insights: activity, topics, your "source diet" for the last 30 days and the mood of your feeds over time.
• Built-in podcast player that keeps playing when the popup closes, at 0.75× to 2× speed.
• Star an item to get its key points and file it in your History.

🎓 RESEARCH MODE AND CITATIONS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Detects academic papers on PubMed, Springer, Wiley, JSTOR and other publishers.
• Correct citations from the DOI in APA 7, MLA 9, Chicago (author-date), BibTeX and RIS. Citation data comes from doi.org, never from the AI, and only the DOI leaves your device.
• Download references.bib or .ris for Zotero, Mendeley or LaTeX.
• Source check for any page: type, author, date and publisher, plus four lateral-reading questions to judge a source before you trust it. Hints, not ratings.
• A Research tab with filters for reviews, trials and preprints.

☁️ YOUR LIBRARY ON YOUR OWN STORAGE (NEW)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Keep your library on all your computers, back it up, or publish part of it, without handing it to anyone. In Settings → Sync & backup, add a place: Nextcloud or another WebDAV server, an S3-compatible bucket or a folder on your computer.

• Encrypted: everything is encrypted in your browser with your passphrase before it leaves the device, so the server only sees scrambled files. You get a recovery key; without it and the passphrase, nobody can read them, including us.
• Two-way sync between your devices, merged field by field. The same page saved twice becomes one article, and a flood of deletions from another device waits for your OK.
• Backup-only places, and restore with one click.
• Publish a list to your own website: pick articles by tag and get plain files plus a viewer page with search, timeline, tag graph and a 3D map of what you saved. Notes and full texts stay out unless you switch them on.
• Off until you add a place. We run no sync server and see nothing.

❓ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Is AI Summary Helper free?
Yes. The extension is free and open source. With your own API key you pay your provider directly, usually a few cents a month. An on-device model costs nothing. byphil Cloud includes a small free daily allowance; Pro removes the limits.

Can I summarize web pages with a local AI model?
Yes. Install Ollama, download an open model and pick the local option in the setup. Everything, including follow-up questions and feed recaps, then runs on your computer, also with a self-hosted Ollama server on your network.

Which AI providers work with my own API key?
OpenAI, Google Gemini, Mistral AI and DeepSeek, plus local and self-hosted models.

Does it work with PDFs and YouTube?
Yes. PDFs opened in Chrome, including files on your computer, and YouTube videos with captions work like any other page.

Can it summarize an English article in another language?
Yes. Choose any of 41 summary languages; the source can be in a different language.

How do I send web articles to my Kindle?
Sign in to byphil, add kindle@byphil.eu as an approved sender in your Amazon account, enter your device's email address in Settings → Send, and choose it under Send. Three sends are free; Pro makes it unlimited.

Can I use my library in Claude Desktop or Cursor?
Yes. Turn on AI apps in Settings → AI apps, pair it once, and the app can search your library, quote your highlights and cite sources. It is off by default, runs on your computer and respects what you allow.

Is my data private?
Your library stays in your browser. Page text goes only to the AI provider you picked, or nowhere with an on-device model. The extension contains no tracking SDK, and the code is open for review.

How is this different from other AI summarizer extensions?
Most AI reading tools lock you into one provider, require an account and route everything through their servers on a monthly subscription. AI Summary Helper lets you bring your own key or run fully local, needs no account for either, and turns what you read into a searchable library with a knowledge graph, analytics, RSS and e-reader export.
