How it connects

Your library stays on your computer

A small relay passes requests from your AI app to the extension. The relay stores no library data.

On your computer
  1. Your AI appClaude Desktop, LM Studio, Cursor
  2. stdio
  3. aish-mcp relayForwards requests, keeps no library data
  4. localhost · token checked
  5. AI Summary HelperSearch, storage, consent and activity log

Off by default: you switch it on in Settings and can revoke it at any time. Only what your AI app sends to its own model leaves your computer.
