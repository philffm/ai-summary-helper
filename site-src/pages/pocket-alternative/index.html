How it works

Save it, understand it, read it where you want

1. Save in one click

Open any article, press the button, and it is in your library with a summary and tags. Search it later, even by a phrase you half remember.

2. Read the summary first

A short summary appears beside the article. Ask a follow-up and the answer points to the passage it came from, so you can check it.

3. Get a digest of your pile

Tap Digest: it picks five of your saves and sends them as one bundle to your Kindle or a device on your network. The first three Kindle sends are included.

The pictures on this page are AI-generated illustrations, not photos of customers.

Saved a link on your phone? The Android companion (Beta) adds “AISH” to the share sheet, and the link waits in your browser's History for one tap.

Questions

Before you switch

Do I need an account?

No. Install it, bring your own AI provider key (or use a local Ollama model), and start saving. An account only matters if you want byphil Cloud.

Does it cost anything?

No. Everything works for free with your own key or a local model. A donation (the Support Pass) unlocks byphil Cloud models and unlimited Kindle sends.

What leaves my browser?

Only the text you ask to summarize, sent to the AI provider you chose. With a local model nothing leaves your machine. Your library itself stays on your device.

Can I send articles to my Kindle?

Yes. Digest bundles five of your saves and sends them to your Kindle or a device on your network. The first three Kindle sends are included; unlimited sends need the Support Pass.

What if I stop using it, or you stop?

Your library is stored in your browser and can be exported, so a shutdown of the service cannot take it away. The code is open source.

Can I use it on my phone?

Saving needs the browser extension on a computer. On Android, the companion app (Beta) lets you share links to it from your phone. iOS is not available yet.
