Questions

Before you install

Search the answers by topic, provider, privacy, archive or platform.

Do I need an account to use it?

No. Install it, bring your own AI provider key or use a local Ollama model, and start saving. An account is only needed for optional byphil Cloud access or extra Kindle/LocalSend sends.

What actually leaves my browser?

Only the summarization request goes to the AI provider you configured. RSS also needs requests to your subscribed feeds; an AI recap sends headlines and short snippets only when you request it. Search, duplicate detection, tags and recommendations run locally.

Where is my reading archive stored?

By default, in your browser on your device. You can export it as Markdown or back it up encrypted to your own Nextcloud, WebDAV server, S3 bucket or local folder.

What happens if I clear my browser data?

Local browser data can remove a local archive if you have no backup. Export your library or configure encrypted sync to your own storage before clearing browser data or changing profiles.

Which AI providers and models are supported?

You can use OpenAI, Gemini, Mistral, DeepSeek, a compatible custom endpoint, or a local Ollama model. Bring your own key, or use optional byphil Cloud access.

Can I summarize PDFs, YouTube videos and RSS items?

Yes. Summarize PDFs opened in the browser or attached from disk, YouTube videos through their captions, and articles from the built-in RSS reader.

How reliable are the AI summaries?

AI output can be wrong and does not replace checking the source. Follow-up answers show the passage they rely on, so you can compare the summary with the original page.

Which browsers and platforms are supported?

Use the browser extension, native side panel or bookmarklet. An Android companion adds links from your phone; iOS is not available yet.

What happens to my saves if I stop supporting the project?

Your archive stays exactly where it's always been — in your browser's local storage on your device. The Support Pass only gates cloud-dependent extras (byphil Cloud AI access, unlimited Kindle/LocalSend sends), never access to what you've already saved.

Is this open source?

Yes — the extension's source is public on GitHub, under the AGPL-3.0 license. It is free for personal, educational and non-profit use; companies that want to use it outside the AGPL's terms can get a commercial license.
