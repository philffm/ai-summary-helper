# Global transcreation brief

## Purpose

Adapt meaning, tone, rhythm and persuasive intent for each market, not only
the individual words.

## Apply transcreation to

- Hero and section headlines
- Marketing CTAs
- Blog and landing-page titles
- Light humour, metaphors and wordplay
- Benefit framing and other persuasive copy

## Do not transcreate

- Legal, privacy or security claims
- Technical instructions and data-flow descriptions
- Exact product and feature names
- Regulatory, compliance or evidence-based wording

## Rules

1. Preserve intent rather than literal wording.
2. Prefer natural phrasing in the target language.
3. Adapt idioms and wordplay by function when a literal translation would lose
   the effect.
4. Use humour only when it is natural and appropriate for the target market.
5. Never invent facts, features, guarantees or privacy claims.
6. Keep evidence-based claims conservative.
7. Preserve the hierarchy between primary and secondary CTAs.
8. When a cultural adaptation is uncertain, choose clear neutral phrasing.
