Get the bookmarklet

Works on any browser and OS — including iOS, where the extension can't run. Pick a provider, generate a link, and drag it to your bookmarks bar. One click on any page inserts an AI summary right into it.

Use your byPhil account — no API key to manage, and most LLM models are available through the byPhil API. The bookmarklet calls the byPhil Cloud API with your session.
